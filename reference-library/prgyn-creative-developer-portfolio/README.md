## What It Is
`prgyn.dev` is an award-winning creative developer portfolio designed as a **7-chapter narrative journey** (`blank`, `spark`, `sketch`, `code`, `lab`, `alive`, `next`), culminating in a theatrical colophon footer (`#credits`).

Rather than relying on static case study cards or embedded video recordings, the portfolio renders every interaction in **100% real-time client-side math and physics**:
- An interactive Catmull-Rom scribble recognition engine.
- A 6-geometry wireframe classifier.
- Generative mathematical art stages (Fibonacci spirals, harmonographs, phyllotaxis).
- Velocity-sensitive 3D plane banking.
- 7 physics lab rooms (including Matter.js 2D rigid-body simulation and 3D Fibonacci sphere distribution).
- A 2-phase retro CRT cathode-ray power-on sequence.
- A 3D origami letter folded via Quaternion kinematics and affine canvas warping.
- A 24 FPS vintage film projection grain generator with optical lens focal blur.

---

## Why It's Exceptional

1. **Zero Pre-Rendered Video Overhead:**
   Every fluid visual is executed live in DOM/Canvas at the user's native screen refresh rate (60Hz, 120Hz, or 240Hz). This guarantees instant loading and infinite resolution.
2. **Tactile Proximity Physics:**
   Typography is not static text; using variable font axes (`wght`, `wdth`), text subtly stretches, fattens, and tracks pointer proximity with smooth lerp interpolation.
3. **Seamless State Hand-offs:**
   Transitions between acts are physical: the preloader does not merely fade out; its signature cursor morphs and physically flies across the viewport into the hero editor caret.
4. **Adaptive 6-Theme Design System:**
   The entire site shifts smoothly through 6 cohesive themes (`theme-ink`, `theme-paper`, `theme-blueprint`, `theme-terminal`, `theme-mint`, `theme-crimson`), maintaining consistent contrast and layout grids.

## Best Fit Scenarios
- High-end design agency websites and creative technologist portfolios.
- Product launch landing pages requiring immersive storytelling and tactile interaction.
- Interactive educational tools and generative art showcases.
- Motion-rich micro-interactions (e.g., proximity typography, physics tags, CRT modal reveals).

## When to Avoid
- Data-heavy web apps, dashboards, or productivity software where immediate scanability is paramount.
- Ultra-low-bandwidth or battery-constrained environments where multiple WebGL/Canvas contexts could degrade performance.
- Projects with strict standard form UX requirements (e.g. government or healthcare portals).
