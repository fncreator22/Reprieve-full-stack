## 📌 Best Suited For
1. **Theatrical & Agency Hero Stages:** When a product or portfolio requires an unforgettable narrative opening, such as the preloader signature handing off its caret to the hero title.
2. **Tactile Micro-Interactions:** Variable fonts reacting to pointer proximity, velocity-sensitive scroll banking, or magnetic hover force fields.
3. **Interactive Demonstrations & Playgrounds:** Embedded physics sandboxes (rigid bodies, 3D spheres, spline smoothers).
4. **Cinematic Theatrical Footers:** 24 FPS throttled film grain with distance-from-center optical lens blur.

## ⚠️ Avoid When
1. **Utility-Dense SaaS Dashboards:** High-density enterprise data grids will be impeded by continuous canvas rendering, large typography shifts, and 3D plane rotations.
2. **Text-Heavy Documentation & Blogs:** Content requiring fast scanning, copy-pasting, and standard document flow should not be locked behind scroll-pinned canvas stages.
3. **Low-Power Mobile Scenarios without Performance Guards:** If target devices include low-end mobile hardware, multiple concurrent canvas loops and SVG displacement filters will trigger battery drain and thermal throttling unless properly unmounted via `IntersectionObserver`.
